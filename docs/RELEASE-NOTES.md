# Dropzone 2.4.0

Rocket League live tracking, player profiles, and friends are now available.

- Automatic game setup, live scoreboards, and a post-match breakdown that stays until the next match.
- Match history shows game modes, your team’s score, results, and aligned personal stats. Free Play is excluded from recording and history.
- Personal and friend profiles include match records, performance charts, and supported personal stats.
- Free accounts include email verification, password recovery, profile pictures, friends, and a rolling year of cloud match history. Local mode keeps the last 30 days on this PC.
- Cleaner game navigation and tracker settings. The profile sidebar can be hidden and remembers your choice; its artwork fades into the stats below.
- Fixed single-day chart sizing, signup validation, and local storage errors.

Stats are recorded by Dropzone; competitive rank and MMR are not supplied by the game’s Stats API. Existing unknown game modes are labeled as unavailable rather than guessed.
