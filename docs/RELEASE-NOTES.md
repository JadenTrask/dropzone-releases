# Dropzone 2.5.1

- **Smoother settings and Rocket League navigation:** saving launch preferences no longer shifts the page, and internal Rocket League transitions leave the glass navigation header stable. Accessible save announcements and visible error recovery remain available.
- **Consistent Videos pages:** Call of Duty, Apex, Siege, League of Legends, THE FINALS and Rocket League now share the same glass layout, search, filters, playback controls, loading and recovery states. Scrolling and Show more preserve the current workspace position.
- **Rocket League Videos:** official RL Esports uploads, broadcasts and playlists are available from the existing top navigation. Official Siege media snapshots are also bundled for initial loading.

The newly supplied game-logo backgrounds are still pending and are not included in this release. Existing artwork and the approved clear-glass material are preserved. Gray Zone Warfare remains **Under construction**.

The full Node suite and focused headless renderer, transition and settings checks passed; details and limits are recorded in docs/VALIDATION.md. Native installation, installed-version upgrade, streamed video playback and real gameplay were not exercised in this pass. The Windows installer remains unsigned. No provider keys or private validation fixtures are shipped.
